1.1 Introduction to Drawing Instruments & Sheets — ड्राइंग उपकरण और शीट

English: Drawing instruments include drawing boards, mini-drafter/T-square, compass, divider, set-squares, and pencils. Standard drawing sheet sizes follow BIS specifications (e.g., A0, A1, A2, A3, A4).

हिंदी: Drawing instruments में drawing board, mini-drafter, compass, divider, set-squares और pencils शामिल हैं। Drawing sheets के layout और sizes BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) specifications के अनुसार निर्धारित होते हैं (जैसे A0 से A4).

1.2 Types of Lines as per BIS — BIS के अनुसार विभिन्न प्रकार की रेखाएं

English: Different line types are used for visible outlines, hidden edges, center lines, dimension lines, and cutting planes as per BIS standards.

हिंदी: BIS specifications के अनुसार visible outlines, hidden edges, center lines, dimension lines और cutting planes के लिए अलग-अलग line styles और thickness का उपयोग किया जाता है.

1.3 Geometrical Figures and Lettering — ज्यामितीय आकृतियाँ और लेटरिंग

English: Construction of triangles, rectangles, polygons (pentagon, hexagon), circles, ellipses, and curves using drawing instruments. Lettering includes single-stroke vertical and inclined capital letters (ratios of 7:4 in series like 5, 8, 12 mm).

हिंदी: Drawing instruments की सहायता से triangles, rectangles, hexagons, pentagons, circles और ellipses बनाना। Lettering में single-stroke vertical और inclined capital letters (7:4 अनुपात में 5, 8, 12 mm सीरीज़) का अभ्यास किया जाता है.

Lettering Ratio: Height to Width ratio is standard 7:4.

1.4 Dimensioning Principles — विमांकन (Dimensioning)

English: Dimensioning is essential for specifying sizes, locations, angles, radii, and features like chamfered surfaces, threaded holes, and P.C.D. (Pitch Circle Diameter).

हिंदी: Objects की exact size और location दर्शाने के लिए dimensioning आवश्यक है। इसमें overall sizes, circles, angles, tapered surfaces, holes, P.C.D., countersunk और counterbored holes के लिए standard principles अपनाए जाते हैं.

1.5 Scales — मापनी (Scales)

English: Scales are needed to draw large objects on small sheets or vice versa. Representative Fraction (R.F.) is the ratio of drawing size to actual size. Types include plain and diagonal scales.

हिंदी: बड़े objects को sheet पर fit करने या छोटे parts को बड़ा दिखाने के लिए scales की आवश्यकता होती है। Representative Fraction (R.F.) drawing size और actual size का ratio होता है। Plain और diagonal scales इसके प्रमुख प्रकार हैं.

R.F. = Length of object on drawing / Actual length of object

2.1 Projection of Points and Lines — बिंदुओं और रेखाओं के प्रक्षेप

English: Projections of points situated in different quadrants. Projection of straight lines parallel to both planes, perpendicular to one plane and inclined to the other.

हिंदी: विभिन्न quadrants में स्थित points के projections। Straight lines के projections—जब रेखाएं दोनों planes के parallel हों, किसी एक plane के perpendicular हों या inclined हों.

2.2 Projection of Planes — तलों के प्रक्षेप

English: Projections of various laminas such as square, rectangular, triangular, and circular planes inclined to one plane and parallel/perpendicular to another, strictly in 1st angle projection.

हिंदी: Square, rectangular, triangular और circular laminas (तलों) के projections जब वे एक plane से inclined और दूसरे के parallel/perpendicular हों (केवल 1st angle projection में).

2.3 Orthographic Views of Objects — वस्तुओं के ऑर्थोग्राफिक दृश्य

English: Three views (Front View, Top View, Side View) of different engineering objects to identify surfaces and spatial details.

हिंदी: विभिन्न engineering objects के तीन मुख्य दृश्य (Front View, Top View, Side View) खींचना और उनकी surfaces को पहचानना.

3.1 Projection of Solids — ठोसों के प्रक्षेप

English: Definition and types of solids (prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones, spheres). Drawing their top view, front view, and side view in different orientations.

हिंदी: Solids की परिभाषा और प्रकार (prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones आदि)। विभिन्न orientations में उनके Top View, Front View और Side View बनाना.

3.2 Sections of Solids — ठोसों के खंड (Sections)

English: Drawing of full section, half section, partial/broken-out sections, offset sections, revolved sections, and removed sections. Conventional sectional representation of materials and conventional breaks for shafts, pipes, and structural steel sections.

हिंदी: Full section, half section, partial sections, offset sections और revolved/removed sections बनाना। विभिन्न materials के लिए sectional representation और shafts, pipes, channels पर conventional breaks दिखाना.

4.1 Isometric Scale and Views — आइसोमेट्रिक स्केल और दृश्य

English: Difference between natural scale and isometric scale. Construction of isometric view and isometric projection for objects containing lines, circles, and arcs.

हिंदी: Natural scale और isometric scale में अंतर। Lines, circles और arcs से युक्त वस्तुओं के लिए isometric view और isometric projection तैयार करना.

Isometric Length = 0.816 × True Length

4.2 Conversion of Views — ऑर्थोग्राफिक से आइसोमेट्रिक रूपांतरण

English: Converting given orthographic views (Front and Top views) into a unified 3D isometric view or projection.

हिंदी: दिए गए 2D orthographic views (Front और Top views) को 3D isometric view या projection में बदलना.

5.1 Basic Commands and Interface — बेसिक कमांड्स और इंटरफ़ेस

English: Basic introduction, workspace, and operational instructions of various drafting and editing commands in AutoCAD.

हिंदी: AutoCAD का बेसिक परिचय, workspace और विभिन्न drafting तथा editing commands के संचालन निर्देश.

5.2 2D CAD Drawing Sheets — 2D सीएडी ड्राइंग शीट

English: Creating at least two sheets in AutoCAD covering 3D geometric solids like cube, cuboid, cone, pyramid, truncated cone, pyramid, sphere, and their combinations.

हिंदी: AutoCAD में cube, cuboid, cone, pyramid, truncated cone, sphere और उनके combination पर आधारित कम से कम दो drawing sheets तैयार करना। (Note: AutoCAD drawings are evaluated internally via sessional marks).

Evaluation Note: As per BTEUP guidelines, AutoCAD drawings are evaluated internally through sessional marks rather than final theory examination.
IMPORTANT PRACTICAL / APPLIED TOPICS

स्रातक/डिप्लोमा पाठ्यक्रम के अनुसार निम्नलिखित practical drawing sheets और CAD अभ्यास महत्वपूर्ण हैं:

Sheet Contents

Single-stroke vertical and inclined lettering (7:4 ratio), types of lines as per BIS, and construction of regular polygons, ellipses, and curves.

Drawing plain and diagonal scales with R.F. calculations and practicing dimensioning systems on engineering components.

Orthographic projection problems of points in various quadrants, lines inclined to reference planes, and planes/lamina.

Projections of prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones, and drawing sectional views (full/half sections) with conventional material hatching.

Conversion of given orthographic views into isometric views using isometric scales.

Computer-aided drafting sheets of 3D solids (cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere, pyramid) using AutoCAD commands.
