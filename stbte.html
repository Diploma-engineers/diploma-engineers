About State Technical Education Board

Technical Education & Diploma Board

State Technical Education Board state ke andar technical institutions, polytechnics aur diploma courses ko regulate karne wali pramukh statutory body hai.

Board ke antargat Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Science aur anya engineering branches mein diploma courses sanchalit kiye jaate hain.

Students ke liye academic calendar, curriculum, exam form submission, results, aur sabhi official updates board ke portal par samay-samay par jari kiye jaate hain.
