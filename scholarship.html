How to Apply

Simple step-by-step flow for students to understand the process quickly.

1

Registration

Start new application or renewal on the official scholarship portal.

2

Fill Student Details

Enter personal, academic, bank, and institute information carefully.

3

Upload Documents

Attach the correct scanned files in the required format and size.

4

Institute Verification

Your college checks the form, documents, and fee details.

5

District / Office Verification

Application moves to the next verification stage after college approval.

6

Scholarship Approved

If all details are correct, payment is processed to your bank account.
