COURSE CONTENTS — Mathematics - II | BTEUP Course 2.1

Syllabus Overview: This page covers all 5 units of BTEUP Mathematics-II including Determinants & Matrices, Integral Calculus, Differential Equations & Numerical Methods (Trapezoidal, Simpson's rules, MATLAB), 2D Coordinate Geometry, and Vector Algebra.

Tip for Students: Practice numerical problems step-by-step. Formulas are highlighted for quick revision during exams.

1. Determinants (सारणिक)

English: Elementary properties of determinants up to 3rd order. Consistency of linear equations and Cramer's Rule for solving simultaneous equations.

हिंदी: 3rd order तक के सारणिक के गुणधर्म, समीकरणों की संगति (Consistency), और क्रैमर का नियम (Cramer's Rule)।

Cramer's Rule: x = Dx/D, y = Dy/D, z = Dz/D (where D != 0)

2. Matrices (आव्यूह)

English: Algebra of matrices (addition, multiplication), transpose, inverse of a matrix, and matrix inverse method to solve a system of linear equations in three variables.

हिंदी: आव्यूह बीजगणित, प्रतिलोम आव्यूह (Inverse), और आव्यूह विधि द्वारा तीन चरों वाले रैखिक समीकरण निकाय को हल करना।

Matrix Inverse Method: X = A^(-1) * B, where A^(-1) = adj(A) / |A|

1. Methods of Integration

English: Integration as inverse of differentiation. Simple integration by substitution, integration by parts, and partial fractions (linear factors only).

हिंदी: अवकलन के विपरीत प्रक्रिया के रूप में समाकलन। प्रतिस्थापन द्वारा, खंडशः (By parts), और आंशिक भिन्न (Partial fractions) द्वारा समाकलन।

Integration by Parts: ∫ u v dx = u ∫ v dx - ∫ [ (du/dx) ∫ v dx ] dx

2. Definite Integrals & Reduction Formulae

English: Introduction to definite integration and standard reduction formulae:

∫[0 to π/2] sin^n x dx, ∫[0 to π/2] cos^n x dx, ∫[0 to π/2] sin^m x cos^n x dx

3. Applications of Integration

English: Finding area bounded by a curve and axes; calculating volume of a solid formed by revolution of an area about axes.

हिंदी: वक्र और अक्षों के बीच घिरे क्षेत्रफल का निर्धारण और वक्र के परिक्रमण (Revolution) से बने ठोस का आयतन ज्ञात करना।

1. Differential Equations

English: Definition, order and degree of differential equations, formation of differential equations, and solution of first order and first degree differential equations by variable separable method.

हिंदी: अवकल समीकरण की परिभाषा, कोटि (Order) और घात (Degree), निर्माण, तथा चर पृथक्करण विधि (Variable Separable Method) द्वारा हल।

2. Numerical Methods & MATLAB

English: Numerical integration using Trapezoidal rule, Simpson's 1/3 rule, and Simpson's 3/8 rule. Simple introduction to MATLAB commands and environment.

Trapezoidal Rule: I = h/2 [ y_0 + 2(y_1 + y_2 + ... + y_{n-1}) + y_n ]
Simpson's 1/3 Rule: I = h/3 [ (y_0 + y_n) + 4(y_1 + y_3 + ...) + 2(y_2 + y_4 + ...) ]
Simpson's 3/8 Rule: I = 3h/8 [ (y_0 + y_n) + 3(y_1 + y_2 + y_4 + ...) + 2(y_3 + y_6 + ...) ]

1. Straight Lines & Circles

English: Equation of straight lines in various standard forms, angle between two lines, perpendicular distance formula. General equation of a circle, finding circle equation given center & radius, three points, or diameter endpoints.

हिंदी: सरल रेखा के विभिन्न मानक रूप, दो रेखाओं के बीच कोण, लंबवत दूरी। वृत्त का सामान्य समीकरण और विभिन्न स्थितियों में वृत्त का समीकरण ज्ञात करना।

2. Conics (शंकु परिच्छेद)

English: Definition of conics (Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola) and their standard equations. Problems when foci, directrices, or vertices are given.

Parabola: y^2 = 4ax | Ellipse: x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1 | Hyperbola: x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1

1. Vectors and Operations

English: Definition, notation, rectangular resolution of a vector, addition and subtraction. Scalar (dot) product and Vector (cross) product of two vectors.

हिंदी: सदिश की परिभाषा, आयताकार घटक (Rectangular resolution), सदिशों का जोड़-घटाना, अदिश (Dot) और सदिश (Cross) गुणनफल।

Dot Product: a . b = |a||b| cos θ | Cross Product: a × b = |a||b| sin θ n̂

2. Engineering Applications

English: Simple problems related to work done, moment of a force, and angular velocity.

हिंदी: कार्य (Work), बल आघूर्ण (Moment), और कोणीय वेग (Angular velocity) से संबंधित समस्याएं।
