🏛️ About Board of Technical Education Uttar Pradesh

Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh (BTEUP) state ke andar technical institutions, polytechnics aur diploma courses ko regulate aur conduct karne wali pramukh statutory body hai.

Iske antargat Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Science aur anya engineering branches mein diploma courses sanchalit kiye jaate hain. Students ke liye academic calendar, curriculum, exam form submission, results, aur sabhi official updates board ke portal par samay-samay par jari kiye jaate hain.

📋 Important for Students

Examination Schedule

Exam forms, admit cards, practical dates aur theory exam schedules ke liye official notifications ko regularly track karein.

Result Verification

Semester results announce hone ke baad apne marks aur grades ko official board website ke zariye verify karein.

Study Curriculum

Apni specific branch aur current semester ke anusar updated syllabus aur study material ka palan karein.

Regular Updates

Kisi bhi tarah ke academic changes ya urgent notices ke liye board ke latest announcements section par nazar rakhein.
