Introduction

Welcome to Diploma Engineers. We are an educational platform created to provide useful learning resources for diploma and polytechnic engineering students.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you access or use our website, mobile application, courses, study materials and related services.

By accessing Diploma Engineers, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect or receive certain information.

1. Account Information

If you create or use an account, information associated with your account may include:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo, if provided
  • Engineering branch or course information
  • Account-related identifiers

2. Educational Information

When you use our educational services, we may process information related to your selected courses, study materials, purchased courses, learning activity and related preferences.

3. Payment Information

For paid services, payment-related information may be submitted by you or processed through third-party payment providers.

We generally do not need to store complete debit-card, credit-card, banking or UPI credentials on our own servers.

4. Technical Information

Technical information may be automatically generated when you access our services, such as device type, browser information, operating system, approximate usage information and diagnostic information.

How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing educational content and services
  • Managing user accounts
  • Providing access to purchased courses
  • Processing and verifying course requests
  • Maintaining website and application functionality
  • Improving educational resources
  • Responding to user support requests
  • Preventing misuse or unauthorized access
  • Maintaining platform security
  • Communicating important service updates

We aim to collect and use information only when it is reasonably necessary for operating and improving the Diploma Engineers platform.

Cookies and Local Storage

Our website or application may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage or similar mechanisms to provide functionality and remember certain preferences.

These technologies may be used for purposes such as maintaining session information, remembering preferences, improving user experience and supporting application functionality.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some features of the website.

Third-Party Services

Diploma Engineers may use third-party services and technologies to operate certain features.

These services may include hosting, analytics, authentication, cloud storage, payment processing, content delivery and other technical services.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the applicable policies of services you choose to use.

External Links

Our platform may contain links to external websites, applications, social-media platforms, video platforms or other resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

Payments and Course Access

Some courses or services on Diploma Engineers may require payment.

Where applicable, payment information may be processed through a third-party payment provider. We may receive transaction-related information necessary to confirm a payment and provide access to the applicable service.

Manual Payment Verification

If a payment verification system requires a transaction ID, UTR or similar reference number, that information may be submitted by the user for verification.

Such information may be used only for confirming the payment request, preventing duplicate or fraudulent claims and enabling the appropriate course access.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission, website, application or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also protect their account credentials and avoid sharing passwords or authentication information with others.

Children's Privacy

Diploma Engineers is an educational platform primarily intended for students and learners.

We do not knowingly seek to collect unnecessary personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information unnecessarily, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

Requests may be subject to verification and applicable legal or technical requirements.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
