1.1 Concept of Angles and Measurement Systems

Angle (कोण) का निर्माण एक initial ray को axis के परितः rotate करके होता है। Rotating direction counter-clockwise होने पर angle positive और clockwise होने पर negative माना जाता है।

  • Degree Measure (सष्टक पद्धति): 1 complete rotation = 360°, 1° = 60' (minutes), 1' = 60" (seconds).
  • Radian Measure (वृत्तीय पद्धति): Radius $r$ वाले circle के arc length $l$ द्वारा center पर बना angle $\theta = l/r$ radians होता है।
  • Conversion Formula: $180^\circ = \pi\text{ radians} \implies 1^\circ = \frac{\pi}{180}\text{ rad}, \quad 1\text{ rad} = \left(\frac{180}{\pi}\right)^\circ \approx 57^\circ 17' 45''$.

1.2 T-Ratios of Allied Angles

Allied angles वे कोण होते हैं जिनका sum या difference $\frac{\pi}{2}$ का integral multiple ($\pm n\frac{\pi}{2} \pm \theta$) होता है।

QuadrantAngle RangePositive T-Ratios (ASTC Rule)
I Quadrant$0^\circ \text{ to } 90^\circ$All T-Ratios Positive (A)
II Quadrant$90^\circ \text{ to } 180^\circ$$\sin\theta, \csc\theta$ Positive (S)
III Quadrant$180^\circ \text{ to } 270^\circ$$\tan\theta, \cot\theta$ Positive (T)
IV Quadrant$270^\circ \text{ to } 360^\circ$$\cos\theta, \sec\theta$ Positive (C)
$\sin(-\theta) = -\sin\theta, \quad \cos(-\theta) = \cos\theta$
$\sin(90^\circ - \theta) = \cos\theta, \quad \cos(90^\circ - \theta) = \sin\theta$
$\sin(180^\circ - \theta) = \sin\theta, \quad \cos(180^\circ - \theta) = -\cos\theta$

1.3 Compound Angle Formulae (Sum & Difference)

दो या अधिक angles के sum तथा difference के trigonometric ratios निम्नलिखित सूत्रों द्वारा व्यक्त किए जाते हैं:

$\sin(A \pm B) = \sin A \cos B \pm \cos A \sin B$
$\cos(A \pm B) = \cos A \cos B \mp \sin A \sin B$
$\tan(A \pm B) = \frac{\tan A \pm \tan B}{1 \mp \tan A \tan B}$

1.4 Transformation Formulae (Product to Sum/Difference & Vice Versa)

Product to Sum/Difference:
$2\sin A \cos B = \sin(A+B) + \sin(A-B)$
$2\cos A \sin B = \sin(A+B) - \sin(A-B)$
$2\cos A \cos B = \cos(A+B) + \cos(A-B)$
$2\sin A \sin B = \cos(A-B) - \cos(A+B)$
Sum/Difference to Product:
$\sin C + \sin D = 2\sin\left(\frac{C+D}{2}\right)\cos\left(\frac{C-D}{2}\right)$
$\sin C - \sin D = 2\cos\left(\frac{C+D}{2}\right)\sin\left(\frac{C-D}{2}\right)$
$\cos C + \cos D = 2\cos\left(\frac{C+D}{2}\right)\cos\left(\frac{C-D}{2}\right)$
$\cos C - \cos D = 2\sin\left(\frac{C+D}{2}\right)\sin\left(\frac{D-C}{2}\right)$

1.5 T-Ratios of Multiple (2A) and Sub-Multiple Angles

$\sin 2A = 2\sin A \cos A = \frac{2\tan A}{1 + \tan^2 A}$
$\cos 2A = \cos^2 A - \sin^2 A = 2\cos^2 A - 1 = 1 - 2\sin^2 A = \frac{1 - \tan^2 A}{1 + \tan^2 A}$
$\tan 2A = \frac{2\tan A}{1 - \tan^2 A}$

1.6 Standard Graphs: $|x|$, $\sin x$, $\cos x$, and $e^x$

  • Modulus Function $y = |x|$: V-shaped graph passing through origin, symmetric about y-axis ($y = x$ for $x \ge 0$, $y = -x$ for $x < 0$). Domain $\mathbb{R}$, Range $[0, \infty)$.
  • Sine Wave $y = \sin x$: Wave graph bounded between $-1$ and $+1$ with period $2\pi$. Continuous curve passing through $(0,0)$.
  • Cosine Wave $y = \cos x$: Wave graph bounded between $-1$ and $+1$ with period $2\pi$. Peak at $(0,1)$.
  • Exponential Function $y = e^x$: Always positive strictly increasing curve passing through $(0,1)$. As $x \to -\infty, y \to 0$.

2.1 Concept of Functions and Limits

Function (फलन): यदि दो variables $x$ और $y$ इस प्रकार संबंधित हों कि $x$ के प्रत्येक मान के लिए $y$ का एक निश्चित unique value मिले, तो $y = f(x)$ कहते हैं।

Limit (सीमा): जब $x$ किसी value $a$ की ओर approach करता है ($x \to a$), तो $f(x)$ जिस निश्चित मान $L$ की ओर approach करता है, उसे limit कहते हैं: $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$.

2.2 Standard Limits

Syllabus में निर्धारित चार मानक सीमाएँ (Standard Limits):

1. $\lim_{x \to a} \frac{x^n - a^n}{x - a} = n a^{n-1}$
2. $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1 \quad (x \text{ in radians})$
3. $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{a^x - 1}{x} = \log_e a \quad (\text{Note: } \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{x} = 1)$
4. $\lim_{x \to 0} (1 + x)^{1/x} = e$

2.3 Differentiation by First Principle (प्रथम सिद्धांत से अवकलन)

First principle derivative formula:

$\frac{dy}{dx} = f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$

Standard functions derived using First Principle:

Function $f(x)$Derivative $\frac{d}{dx}f(x)$
$x^n$$n x^{n-1}$
$\sin x$$\cos x$
$\cos x$$-\sin x$
$\tan x$$\sec^2 x$
$e^x$$e^x$
$\log_e x$$\frac{1}{x}$

2.4 Differentiation Rules (Sum, Product, Quotient & Chain Rule)

Sum/Difference Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}(u \pm v) = \frac{du}{dx} \pm \frac{dv}{dx}$
Product Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}(u \cdot v) = u \frac{dv}{dx} + v \frac{du}{dx}$
Quotient Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{u}{v}\right) = \frac{v \frac{du}{dx} - u \frac{dv}{dx}}{v^2}$
Chain Rule (Function of Function): If $y = f(u)$ and $u = g(x)$, then $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dx}$

2.5 Logarithmic & Exponential Differentiation

जब variable base तथा power दोनों में उपस्थित हो (जैसे $y = [f(x)]^{g(x)}$) या complex product/quotient expression हो, तब दोनों पक्षों में $\log_e$ लेकर अवकलन किया जाता है।

If $y = u^v$, take log: $\log y = v \log u$
Differentiating w.r.t $x$: $\frac{1}{y} \frac{dy}{dx} = v \cdot \frac{1}{u}\frac{du}{dx} + \log u \cdot \frac{dv}{dx} \implies \frac{dy}{dx} = y \left[\frac{v}{u}\frac{du}{dx} + \log u \frac{dv}{dx}\right]$

3.1 Polynomial Fractions, Proper & Improper Fractions

An expression of the form $\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}$ where $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ are polynomials and $Q(x) \neq 0$ is a Polynomial Fraction (बीजीय भिन्न).

  • Proper Fraction (उचित भिन्न): Degree of numerator $P(x)$ < Degree of denominator $Q(x)$. Example: $\frac{x+2}{x^2+3x+2}$.
  • Improper Fraction (अनुचित भिन्न): Degree of numerator $P(x)$ $\ge$ Degree of denominator $Q(x)$. Example: $\frac{x^3+1}{x^2-1}$. Improper fraction को polynomial division द्वारा $P(x)/Q(x) = T(x) + \frac{R(x)}{Q(x)}$ के रूप में बदलकर proper fraction में convert किया जाता है।

3.2 Resolution into Partial Fractions: Non-Repeated Linear Factors

जब $Q(x)$ में भिन्न-भिन्न (non-repeated) एकघातीय (linear) गुणनखंड हों, अर्थात् $Q(x) = (ax+b)(cx+d)$:

$\frac{P(x)}{(ax+b)(cx+d)} = \frac{A}{ax+b} + \frac{B}{cx+d}$

Constants $A$ और $B$ को compare करके अथवा roots substitute करके ज्ञात करते हैं।

3.3 Resolution into Partial Fractions: Repeated Linear Factors

जब $Q(x)$ में पुनरावृत्त (repeated) linear factors हों, अर्थात् $(ax+b)^n$ मौजूद हो:

$\frac{P(x)}{(ax+b)^2(cx+d)} = \frac{A}{ax+b} + \frac{B}{(ax+b)^2} + \frac{C}{cx+d}$
Method: denominators को multiply करके polynomial coefficients equate किए जाते हैं।

4.1 Permutation and Combination Values ($^nP_r$ & $^nC_r$)

$^nP_r = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!} \quad (\text{Permutation / क्रमचय})$
$^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} \quad (\text{Combination / संचय})$
Properties: $^nC_0 = 1, \quad ^nC_n = 1, \quad ^nC_r = ^nC_{n-r}$

4.2 Binomial Expansion for Positive Integral Index

धनात्मक पूर्णांक घातांक $n$ के लिए द्विपाद प्रमेय का प्रसार (Statement without proof):

$(x + a)^n = ^nC_0 x^n a^0 + ^nC_1 x^{n-1} a^1 + ^nC_2 x^{n-2} a^2 + \dots + ^nC_r x^{n-r} a^r + \dots + ^nC_n x^0 a^n$
  • Total number of terms in expansion of $(x+a)^n$ is $(n+1)$.
  • Sum of exponents of $x$ and $a$ in each term is always equal to $n$.

4.3 General Term, Middle Term and Independent Term

General Term (व्यापक पद): $T_{r+1} = ^nC_r x^{n-r} a^r$
Middle Term (मध्य पद):
• If $n$ is EVEN: Number of terms is odd. Single middle term = $T_{\left(\frac{n}{2} + 1\right)}$.
• If $n$ is ODD: Number of terms is even. Two middle terms = $T_{\left(\frac{n+1}{2}\right)}$ and $T_{\left(\frac{n+3}{2}\right)}$.
Independent Term ( $x$-रहित पद): Put the net power of $x$ in general term $T_{r+1}$ equal to $0$ and solve for $r$.

5.1 Definition, Real and Imaginary Parts

An expression of the form $z = a + ib$ where $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ and $i = \sqrt{-1}$ ($i^2 = -1$) is a Complex Number (सम्मिश्र संख्या).

$\text{Re}(z) = a$ (Real part), $\text{Im}(z) = b$ (Imaginary part).

5.2 Conjugate, Modulus and Amplitude

Conjugate (संयुग्मी): $\bar{z} = a - ib$
Modulus (मापांक): $|z| = r = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$
Amplitude / Argument (कोणांक): $\theta = \arg(z) = \tan^{-1}\left(\left|\frac{b}{a}\right|\right)$ (adjusted according to quadrant of $(a,b)$).

5.3 Representations and Conversions

Cartesian Form: $z = a + ib$

Polar Form: $z = r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)$, where $a = r\cos\theta$ and $b = r\sin\theta$.

Euler's Exponential Form: $z = r e^{i\theta}$.

5.4 Arithmetic Operations on Complex Numbers

Let $z_1 = a_1 + ib_1$ and $z_2 = a_2 + ib_2$:
• Addition: $z_1 + z_2 = (a_1 + a_2) + i(b_1 + b_2)$
• Subtraction: $z_1 - z_2 = (a_1 - a_2) + i(b_1 - b_2)$
• Multiplication: $z_1 z_2 = (a_1 a_2 - b_1 b_2) + i(a_1 b_2 + a_2 b_1)$
• Division: $\frac{z_1}{z_2} = \frac{(a_1 + ib_1)(a_2 - ib_2)}{a_2^2 + b_2^2} = \frac{a_1 a_2 + b_1 b_2}{a_2^2 + b_2^2} + i \frac{a_2 b_1 - a_1 b_2}{a_2^2 + b_2^2}$

5.5 De Moivre’s Theorem and Applications

De Moivre’s Theorem Statement: For any real number $n$, $(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^n = \cos(n\theta) + i\sin(n\theta)$.

Special Cases:
$(\cos\theta - i\sin\theta)^n = \cos(n\theta) - i\sin(n\theta)$
$(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^{-n} = \cos(n\theta) - i\sin(n\theta)$
Application: Finding roots of complex numbers, expanding $\cos n\theta$ and $\sin n\theta$, and solving complex equations.
