Units and Dimensions / मात्रक एवं विमाएँ

Measurement, SI system, dimensions, dimensional analysis, errors and significant figures.

1.1

Need of Measurement in Engineering and Science

Engineering में measurement की आवश्यकता और units की basic understanding.

English Explanation

Measurement is the process of comparing an unknown physical quantity with an accepted standard. Engineering design, manufacturing, testing and quality control require reliable measurements.

A numerical value without a unit is incomplete. For example, saying that a rod is 5 does not communicate whether the length is 5 m, 5 cm or 5 mm.

हिन्दी Explanation

Measurement वह process है जिसमें किसी unknown physical quantity की तुलना किसी accepted standard से की जाती है। Engineering design, manufacturing, testing और quality control में accurate measurement आवश्यक है।

किसी numerical value के साथ unit होना जरूरी है। जैसे rod की length केवल 5 बताना incomplete है; 5 m, 5 cm या 5 mm स्पष्ट करना आवश्यक है।

1.2

Dimensions and Dimensional Formulae

Physical quantities को M, L, T आदि fundamental dimensions के रूप में represent करना।

Dimension: किसी physical quantity को fundamental quantities की powers के रूप में express करने को dimension कहते हैं।

Velocity = displacement / time
[v] = [L T⁻¹]

Acceleration = velocity / time
[a] = [L T⁻²]

Force = mass × acceleration
[F] = [M L T⁻²]

Work = force × displacement
[W] = [M L² T⁻²]

Power = work / time
[P] = [M L² T⁻³]

English

The dimensional formula indicates how a physical quantity depends on fundamental dimensions such as mass, length and time.

हिन्दी

Dimensional formula यह बताता है कि कोई physical quantity mass, length और time जैसी fundamental quantities पर किस power के रूप में depend करती है।

1.3

Principle of Homogeneity of Dimensions

Physical equation के दोनों sides की dimensions समान होनी चाहिए।

Example:
s = ut + 1/2 at²
[s] = L
[ut] = (L T⁻¹)(T) = L
[at²] = (L T⁻²)(T²) = L
Therefore equation is dimensionally homogeneous.

यदि equation के किसी term की dimensions बाकी terms से अलग हो, तो equation dimensional point of view से गलत है।

Dimensional Homogeneity Diagram
1.4

Dimensional Equations and Applications

Unit conversion, equation checking और relation derivation.

Applications / उपयोग

  1. Checking correctness of physical equations.
  2. Deriving relation between physical quantities.
  3. Converting numerical values from one system of units to another.
  4. Finding dimensions of constants.
General relation:
Q = k Aᵃ Bᵇ Cᶜ
Taking dimensions:
[Q] = [A]ᵃ [B]ᵇ [C]ᶜ
Limitation: Dimensional analysis cannot determine numerical dimensionless constants such as 2, 1/2, π and sometimes cannot distinguish quantities having identical dimensions.
1.5

Limitations of Dimensional Analysis

Dimensional method powerful है, लेकिन हर equation derive नहीं कर सकती।

  • Dimensionless constants cannot be determined.
  • Trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions cannot generally be obtained from dimensions alone.
  • Different quantities may have same dimensions, for example work and torque.
  • Exact form of a relation may remain undetermined if several variables have the same dimensional combination.
1.6

Error in Measurement / मापन में त्रुटि

Accuracy, precision, least count, random/systematic errors और error combination.

Accuracy

Closeness of measured value to the true value. अधिक accuracy का अर्थ true value के अधिक पास result.

Precision

Repeated measurements के बीच agreement. High precision का मतलब readings एक-दूसरे के बहुत close हैं।

Types of Errors

Randomअनियमित error; repeated observations से reduce हो सकती है।
Systematicएक निश्चित pattern में error; instrument/calibration से जुड़ी हो सकती है।
GrossHuman mistakes जैसे wrong reading या wrong recording.
Absolute Error = |Measured value − True/Mean value|
Relative Error = Absolute Error / Mean value
Percentage Error = Relative Error × 100

Combination of Errors

For addition/subtraction:
If Z = A ± B, then ΔZ = ΔA + ΔB

For multiplication/division:
If Z = AB or A/B,
ΔZ/Z = ΔA/A + ΔB/B

For powers:
If Z = Aⁿ,
ΔZ/Z = n(ΔA/A)

Significant Figures

  • All non-zero digits are significant.
  • Zeros between non-zero digits are significant.
  • Leading zeros are generally not significant.
  • Trailing zeros after decimal are significant when written intentionally.
  • Rounding should follow the required precision of the calculation.

Unit 1 Quick Revision / त्वरित पुनरावृत्ति

  • SI is the standard system used in engineering.
  • Dimension of force = [M L T⁻²].
  • Dimension of work = [M L² T⁻²].
  • Dimension of power = [M L² T⁻³].
  • Homogeneous equation has same dimensions on both sides.
  • Accuracy and precision are different concepts.
  • Percentage error = relative error × 100.

2.2

Addition and Subtraction of Vectors

Triangle law, parallelogram law और scalar/vector product.

Triangle Law

यदि दो vectors को head-to-tail arrange किया जाए, तो first vector के tail से second vector के head तक का vector resultant होता है।

Parallelogram Law

दो vectors को एक common point से represent करने पर parallelogram का diagonal resultant vector देता है।

For vectors A and B with angle θ:
R = √(A² + B² + 2AB cosθ)
Direction: tan α = (B sinθ)/(A + B cosθ)

Scalar Product

A · B = AB cosθ

Dot product का result scalar होता है। Work में इसका application मिलता है: W = F · s.

Vector Product

A × B = AB sinθ n̂

Cross product का result vector होता है। Torque में इसका application मिलता है: τ = r × F.

2.3

Resolution of Vectors

Vertical component = F sinθ

2.4

Force, Momentum and Conservation of Linear Momentum

Force motion change करता है; momentum moving body की quantity of motion को represent करता है।

Momentum: p = mv
Force: F = dp/dt
For constant mass: F = ma

Law of Conservation of Linear Momentum

यदि system पर external resultant force zero है, तो total linear momentum constant रहता है।

Before collision:
Total momentum = m₁u₁ + m₂u₂

After collision:
Total momentum = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂

Therefore:
m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂

Applications

  • Recoil of gun
  • Rocket propulsion
  • Collision of bodies
  • Jet propulsion
  • Explosive separation problems

Impulse and Applications

Impulse J = F Δt = Δp

जब force बहुत कम समय के लिए लगता है, तब impulse momentum change के बराबर होता है।

  • Hammer striking a nail

Catch करते समय hands पीछे ले जाने से collision time बढ़ता है, इसलिए average force कम हो सकता है।

2.9

Gravitational Force and Kepler's Laws

Masses के बीच attraction और planetary motion के basic laws.

F = G m₁m₂/r²

Kepler's Laws

  1. Planets move in elliptical orbits with the Sun at one focus.
  2. The line joining planet and Sun sweeps equal areas in equal times.
  3. Square of orbital period is proportional to cube of semi-major axis: T² ∝ a³.
इन laws का application satellite motion और planetary motion को समझने में होता है।
2.10

Acceleration Due to Gravity

Earth surface, height और depth के साथ g का variation.

At surface:
g ≈ 9.81 m/s²

At height h (for h << R):
g_h ≈ g(1 − 2h/R)

At depth d (ideal uniform Earth model):
g_d ≈ g(1 − d/R)

Height बढ़ने पर g कम होता है। Earth के अंदर depth बढ़ने पर भी ideal model के अनुसार g कम होता है और centre पर zero तक पहुँचता है।

Work, Power and Energy / कार्य, शक्ति एवं ऊर्जा

Work, friction, kinetic energy, potential energy, conservation and power.

3.1

Work / कार्य

Force के कारण displacement होने पर work done होता है।

W = F s cosθ
PositiveForce और displacement same direction में हों; W > 0.
ZeroDisplacement zero या force perpendicular to displacement; W = 0.
NegativeForce displacement के opposite हो; W < 0.

Examples: gravitational force द्वारा upward lifting में gravity का work negative हो सकता है; circular motion में centripetal force instantaneous displacement के perpendicular होने के कारण zero work करता है।

3.2

Friction / घर्षण

Contact surfaces के relative motion या motion tendency का विरोध करने वाला force.

Modern Concept

Friction microscopic surface irregularities और adhesive interactions सहित कई effects के कारण होता है।

Types

  • Static friction
  • Limiting friction
  • Kinetic / sliding friction
  • Rolling friction
  • Fluid friction
Limiting friction: F_lim = μN
μ = F_lim/N

Reducing Friction

  • Lubrication
  • Ball/roller bearings
  • Polishing appropriate surfaces
  • Streamlined shapes in fluid motion

Engineering Applications

Brakes, clutches, belt drives, bearings, walking, vehicle traction, machine components.

Friction on a Block
3.3

Work Done on Horizontal and Inclined Planes

Rough और smooth surfaces पर object को move करने का work.

Horizontal Smooth Surface

W = F s cosθ

Horizontal Rough Surface

Applied force के साथ friction भी work करता है। Friction का work negative होता है।

Inclined Plane

Object के weight का component slope के along motion को oppose या support कर सकता है।

Weight component along incline = mg sinθ
Normal reaction = mg cosθ [for simple stationary geometry]
3.4

Energy / ऊर्जा

Work करने की capacity को energy कहते हैं।

Kinetic Energy

K.E. = 1/2 mv²

Moving body के पास kinetic energy होती है। Velocity double करने पर kinetic energy four times हो जाती है, क्योंकि K.E. ∝ v².

Gravitational Potential Energy

P.E. = mgh

Reference level के respect में position के कारण energy potential energy कहलाती है।

3.5

Mechanical energy = kinetic energy + potential energy.

Mechanical Energy = K.E. + P.E.
E = 1/2 mv² + mgh

Ideal freely falling body में air resistance neglect करने पर total mechanical energy constant रहती है। Potential energy kinetic energy में और kinetic energy potential energy में transform हो सकती है।

5.3

Surface Tension / पृष्ठ तनाव

Liquid surface का minimum area रखने की tendency surface tension से related है।

Concept

Liquid molecules के बीच cohesive forces और liquid-solid adhesive forces surface behavior को affect करते हैं।

Important Terms

  • Cohesive force
  • Adhesive force

Capillary Ascent Formula

5.4

Viscosity and Coefficient of Viscosity

Fluid की internal resistance to relative flow को viscosity कहते हैं।

Stokes' Law

F = 6πηrv

Small spherical body low-speed flow condition में viscous medium से move करते समय Stokes' law useful होता है।

Terminal Velocity

जब downward driving force और upward resistive forces balance हो जाते हैं, body constant velocity से move कर सकती है; इसे terminal velocity कहते हैं।

Engineering Applications

Heat and Thermometry / ऊष्मा एवं तापमिति

Heat, temperature, transfer modes, temperature scales, expansion, thermal conductivity and thermometers.

6.1

Concept of Heat and Temperature

Heat energy transfer है; temperature thermal state का measure है।

Heat / ऊष्मा

Heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference. It is measured in joule in SI system.

Temperature / तापमान

Temperature किसी body की thermal condition बताता है और heat transfer की direction determine करने में मदद करता है। SI unit kelvin है।

Heat and temperature are not the same physical quantity. Heat is transferred energy; temperature is a state variable related to thermal condition.
6.2

Modes of Transfer of Heat

Conduction, convection और radiation.

Conduction Medium के bulk movement के बिना heat transfer. Solids में prominent.
Convection Fluid के bulk movement के साथ heat transfer.
Radiation Electromagnetic radiation द्वारा heat transfer; vacuum में भी हो सकता है।
Three Modes of Heat Transfer

Reducing Heat Losses

  • Use thermal insulation.
  • Reduce unnecessary conduction paths.
  • Use suitable reflective surfaces against radiation.
  • Control convection using insulation and enclosure.
6.3

Temperature Scales and Specific Heat

Celsius, Fahrenheit और Kelvin scales का relationship.

C/5 = (F − 32)/9 = (K − 273.15)/5

Specific Heat Capacity

Q = mcΔT

Specific heat capacity c वह heat है जो unit mass के temperature को unit degree बढ़ाने के लिए required होती है, given conditions में।

6.4

Expansion of Solids, Liquids and Gases

Temperature change के कारण dimensions/volume में change.

Coefficients

Linear expansion: ΔL = αLΔT
Surface expansion: ΔA = βAΔT
Cubical expansion: ΔV = γVΔT

For isotropic solids

β ≈ 2α
γ ≈ 3α

Engineering applications: expansion joints, railway tracks, bridges, pipelines, bimetallic strips और machine design.

6.5

Coefficient of Thermal Conductivity

Material heat conduct करने की ability को thermal conductivity से describe किया जाता है।

Q/t = kA(T₁ − T₂)/L

High k materials heat अच्छे से conduct करते हैं; low k materials thermal insulators की तरह behave करते हैं।

Engineering Applications

  • Building insulation
  • Heat exchangers
  • Boilers and thermal equipment
  • Refrigeration and HVAC insulation
  • Electronic cooling systems
6.6

Types of Thermometers

Mercury thermometer और bimetallic thermometer तथा उनके uses.

Mercury Thermometer

  • Mercury का thermal expansion temperature indication देता है।
  • Glass capillary और calibrated scale का use.
  • Laboratory/educational temperature measurement में traditional use.
  • Mercury toxicity के कारण many applications में alternatives preferred हैं।

Bimetallic Thermometer

  • Two dissimilar metals bonded together.
  • Different expansion rates के कारण strip bends.
  • Mechanical indication या switching applications में useful.
  • Industrial control और thermostatic devices में use.

Selected Numericals / चुनिंदा संख्यात्मक प्रश्न

Formula application की practice के लिए solved examples.

Numerical 1 — Velocity Dimensions

Question: Find the dimensional formula of velocity.

Solution: Velocity = displacement/time.

[v] = [L]/[T] = [L T⁻¹]

Answer: [L T⁻¹]

Numerical 2 — Force

Question: A 5 kg mass accelerates at 3 m/s². Find force.

F = ma = 5 × 3 = 15 N

Answer: 15 N.

Numerical 3 — Momentum

Question: A 2 kg body moves at 8 m/s. Find momentum.

p = mv = 2 × 8 = 16 kg·m/s

Answer: 16 kg·m/s.

Numerical 4 — Kinetic Energy

Question: Find kinetic energy of a 4 kg body moving at 5 m/s.

K.E. = 1/2 mv² = 1/2 × 4 × 25 = 50 J

Answer: 50 J.

Numerical 5 — Power

Question: A machine does 1200 J work in 20 s. Find power.

P = W/t = 1200/20 = 60 W

Answer: 60 W.

Numerical 6 — Torque

Question: A perpendicular force of 50 N acts at 0.4 m from pivot. Find torque.

τ = rF = 0.4 × 50 = 20 N·m

Answer: 20 N·m.

Numerical 7 — Pressure

Question: A force of 200 N acts over area 0.5 m². Find pressure.

P = F/A = 200/0.5 = 400 Pa

Answer: 400 Pa.

Numerical 8 — Heat

Question: A 2 kg material of specific heat 500 J/kg·K is heated through 10 K. Find heat supplied.

Q = mcΔT = 2 × 500 × 10 = 10000 J

Answer: 10,000 J.

Numerical 9 — Temperature Conversion

Question: Convert 40°C to Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

F = 9/5 × 40 + 32 = 104°F
K = 40 + 273.15 = 313.15 K

Answer: 104°F and 313.15 K.

Numerical 10 — Hooke's Law

Question: A spring extends 0.02 m under 4 N force. Find spring constant.

k = F/x = 4/0.02 = 200 N/m

Answer: 200 N/m.
