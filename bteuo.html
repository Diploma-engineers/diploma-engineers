BTEUP (Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh) kya hai?

**Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh (BTEUP)** Lucknow mein sthit ek statutory body hai jo Uttar Pradesh rajya ke andar aane wale sabhi polytechnic and technical institutions ko govern karti hai. Yeh board diploma courses (jaise Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, etc.) ke liye curriculum design karne, examinations conduct karwane, aur marksheets/degrees provide karne ki zimmedari nibhata hai.

Is page par aapko BTEUP se judi saari important direct links milti hain taaki aapko alag-alag jagah bhatakna na pade. Aap free notes, syllabus aur updates ke liye hamare platform ke sath jude reh sakte hain!
