About Anubhav

Founder, learner & digital education creator

Anubhav Gangwar is a Civil Engineering student and the Founder & Developer of Diploma Engineers. His work combines Civil Engineering studies, website development and student-focused digital learning resources. Diploma Engineers is built to bring study material, books, previous-year questions, syllabus, opportunities and career guidance into one accessible platform.


His interests include construction and environment, learning new technologies, building useful web experiences and creating digital solutions that make learning material easier to access.
