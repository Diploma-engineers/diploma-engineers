🏛️ About Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan

Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE) Rajasthan state ke andar school-level education, secondary, senior secondary, aur vocational examinations ko conduct aur regulate karne wali pramukh statutory body hai.

Board dwara har saal Class 10th aur Class 12th ki main examinations sanchalit ki jati hain. Students ke liye academic calendar, curriculum, registration, results, aur sabhi official updates board ke portal par samay-samay par jari kiye jaate hain.

📋 Important for Students

Examination Schedule

Application forms, admit cards, practical dates aur theory exam timetables ke liye official notifications ko regularly track karein.

Result Verification

Board results announce hone ke baad apne marks aur grades ko official board website ke zariye verify karein.

Study Curriculum

Apni class aur current academic year ke anusar updated syllabus aur study guidelines ka palan karein.

Regular Updates

Kisi bhi tarah ke academic changes ya urgent circulars ke liye board ke latest announcements section par nazar rakhein.
