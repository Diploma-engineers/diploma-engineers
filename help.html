1. Understand the App

Know where to begin and how to move between features.

Diploma Engineers is designed to help diploma engineering students find learning resources, prepare for exams, practise questions and stay connected with the student community. Start from the Home screen and use the menu panel for quick access to available sections.

Home

Use Home to discover available resources, course cards, test series and shortcuts.

Menu panel

Open the menu for profile, leaderboard, community, Help / How to Use and other available options.

Browse carefully

Pick the right branch, semester, subject or topic to find relevant content.

Wait for loading

When the loading spinner appears, let the app finish fetching Firebase data before deciding content is missing.

Best way to use the app: Study a topic with notes, revise with Important Questions, practise using Test Series and revisit the topics you found difficult.

2. Login, Register & Set Up Your Profile

Access your account and keep your student details accurate.

Sign in or create an account

  1. Open the Diploma Engineers app and use the login panel.
  2. If you already have an account, enter your registered email and password, or use Google sign-in if it is offered on the screen.
  3. If you are a new user, choose the Register option and complete the requested fields.
  4. After login, open My Profile from the menu and check the details you can edit.

Forgot your password?

  1. On the login panel, enter the email address associated with your account.
  2. Tap Forgot Password? and check your email inbox for the reset message.
  3. Open the genuine password-reset link from Firebase and follow the instructions to set a new password.
  4. If you do not see the message, check Spam/Junk and confirm that the email address is correct.
Account safety: Never share your password, one-time password (OTP), password-reset link or private account details with anyone. Support does not need your password to investigate a problem.

3. Find Branch-wise Notes, Subjects, Syllabus & Books

Navigate from your engineering branch to the exact subject material.

  1. From Home, open the Notes, Books, Syllabus or relevant study-material section.
  2. Select your branch—for example Civil, Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics, Mechanical, IT or Agriculture, depending on the options currently published.
  3. Choose the semester you are studying.
  4. Open the subject or folder. Browse the listed notes, e-books, syllabus documents or other available resources.
  5. Tap an item to open it. Use the available view, download or share controls shown by the page or document viewer.

How to get the most benefit

  • Match the branch and semester with your current syllabus before using a file.
  • Use notes to understand concepts first, then use Important Questions for focused revision.
  • Keep downloaded files organised by semester and subject so they are easy to find later.
  • If a subject or folder appears empty, wait for loading to complete and check your internet connection before retrying.
Remember: Only material that has been uploaded or published for your selected branch/semester can appear. If a resource is missing, send feedback or contact support with its exact name.

4. Important Questions: Open, Read & Download PDF

Use exam-focused question sets for revision and practice.

  1. Open Important Questions from Home or the relevant menu/learning section.
  2. Choose your branch, semester and subject folder.
  3. Select the required Important Questions document or PDF from the available list.
  4. Wait for the document to load, then read it in the viewer. If a Download button is available, use it to save the file.
  5. If the app opens the browser print dialog for a clean PDF, choose Save as PDF, set paper size to A4 and check the preview before saving.
  6. Open the saved file from your device's Downloads folder or file manager.

If the PDF is blurry or does not open

  • Prefer the direct PDF/download option where available; avoid taking screenshots of text pages.
  • Check that the download finished and the file size is not zero.
  • Try opening the file in a trusted PDF viewer or your browser.
  • If a page looks clipped, check print preview, A4 paper size and page margins.
  • If the file itself is low-resolution, report the subject and file name so the team can check the source.
Study tip: Attempt the questions yourself before checking answers or notes. Mark topics you cannot answer and revise those chapters again.

5. Test Series: Start a Test & Review Your Result

Practise in a structured way and learn from each attempt.

Before starting

  1. Open Test Series from Home or the relevant navigation option.
  2. Browse the available branch, semester, subject and topic folders.
  3. Open a published test and check its title, question count, duration and marks if displayed.
  4. Read the instructions carefully. If an instruction/consent screen appears, read it and tap Agree only when you are ready to proceed.

During the test

  1. Read each question completely before selecting an answer.
  2. Use the available navigation controls to move between questions.
  3. Keep an eye on the timer if the test is timed.
  4. Review unanswered questions and selected options before submitting, where the interface allows it.
  5. Use the on-screen Submit/Finish control to complete the test. Avoid refreshing or closing the page during an attempt unless the interface tells you it is safe.

After the test

  1. Review your score and result screen.
  2. Open Analysis or Test History if available to review past attempts and identify topics needing revision.
  3. Revisit the relevant notes and Important Questions, then try another practice test.
  4. If the app offers a result image or share button, check the image before sharing it.
Important: Test history and results can only be shown or saved when that feature is available and the app can successfully save/load the attempt. If a result does not appear, note the test title and report the issue.

6. How to Buy or Unlock a Course

Check course details, complete the shown payment process and track access.

  1. On Home, find the Courses or Unlock Courses section and open the course you want.
  2. Read the course title, description, included semester/topics, available lessons and displayed price carefully.
  3. Tap Buy, Unlock or the equivalent button shown for that course.
  4. Follow the payment instructions presented by the app. Always verify the amount and payment recipient shown before paying.
  5. If the app requests a UTR/transaction reference, enter it accurately and submit the approval request. Keep your payment confirmation until access is working.
  6. Wait for payment verification and course approval. The time required can vary; do not make a second payment just because the first request is still pending.
  7. When the course becomes unlocked, reopen it and select the semester, folder or lesson you want to study.
Payment safety: Do not send your password or OTP to anyone. Never pay a person or account that does not match the payment instructions displayed in the official app. Contact support if money was deducted but the status is unclear.

If you paid but the course is still locked

  • Check that you are signed in with the same account used for the purchase request.
  • Reopen the course and refresh/retry after a short wait.
  • Prepare the course name, payment date and transaction/UTR reference for support.
  • Do not share complete card details, banking passwords or OTPs while asking for help.

Course prices, payment methods and approval steps may vary depending on the current course setup. Follow the instructions actually displayed on the course page.

7. Play Course Videos & Open Lesson Material

Open the right lesson and troubleshoot common playback issues.

  1. First confirm that the course is unlocked for your account.
  2. Open the course and select the correct semester or lesson folder.
  3. Tap the video title/thumbnail to open the player.
  4. Press the Play button. Use Pause, volume, seek bar or Full Screen controls if available.
  5. If lessons include PDFs or notes, open those from the lesson/folder list separately.
  6. After studying a video, write down key concepts and solve related questions to reinforce learning.

Video troubleshooting

Video keeps buffering

Check the network, wait briefly and try a more stable connection. Video quality and loading speed depend partly on the video host and your internet.

Play button does nothing

Tap once again, reopen the lesson and make sure the course is unlocked. If it opens an external player, follow that player's controls.

Video is locked

Check course approval and ensure you are logged in to the correct account. Contact support if the purchase was approved but access is still unavailable.

Full screen issue

Try rotating your device if appropriate, use the player's full-screen control and exit full screen with the player/browser control.

8. Feedback: Why It Is Important & How to Submit It

Your feedback helps improve the experience for the whole student community.

Feedback is not just a rating. It tells the team what is useful, what is confusing and what should be fixed or added. Clear feedback can help the team prioritise missing resources, broken buttons, PDF quality, video access and other improvements.

  1. Open the app's Feedback option or feedback form, wherever available.
  2. Select the star rating that best matches your experience.
  3. Write a specific message. Mention the page or feature and explain what happened.
  4. For a missing resource, include the branch, semester, subject and material name.
  5. For a technical issue, explain the steps you took and include the exact error message if one appeared.
  6. Submit the form once and wait for the confirmation shown by the app.

Examples of helpful feedback

Helpful feedback

“Civil Engineering, Semester 4, Surveying Important Questions PDF opens but page 2 is clipped when saved as A4.”

Less useful feedback

“App is bad.” This does not explain what happened or which feature needs attention.

Why your feedback matters: It helps identify real student needs, improve reliability and make study resources easier to find. Be honest, respectful and specific. Never include passwords, OTPs or banking credentials.

9. Leaderboard, Coins & Daily Streak

Use participation features to stay motivated and consistent.

The Leaderboard can show student rankings based on the activity or scoring rules configured in the app. Coins or streaks, where enabled, can help you notice regular participation and celebrate progress.

  1. Open Leaderboard from the menu or tap the relevant ranking/points card if one is shown.
  2. Review your position and the displayed ranking details.
  3. Check the app's on-screen descriptions to understand which activities contribute to points or streaks.
  4. Use the leaderboard as encouragement to practise regularly—not as a replacement for learning or a reason to compare yourself negatively with others.
What is the benefit? A leaderboard can make progress visible, encourage healthy consistency and create a friendly learning community. Your main goal should remain understanding your subjects and improving your own results.

Rankings, coins and streak values depend on the current app configuration and the data successfully saved to your account. If a value appears incorrect, report it with the page name and a brief explanation.

10. Share Diploma Engineers with Friends

Help other diploma students discover learning resources.

  1. Use the app's Share option if available, or tap Share Guide at the top of this page.
  2. Choose WhatsApp, Telegram, Messages or another sharing app supported by your device.
  3. Select classmates or friends who may find the learning resources useful.
  4. If the share sheet does not open, copy the official app link and paste it into a message.

Sharing the app can help classmates discover branch-wise study material, Important Questions, available Test Series and course information. It can also help students find a single starting point for their study resources and learn about new features.

Share responsibly: Share the official app link rather than an unknown download file. Do not promise a reward or benefit unless it is explicitly described in the app's current official terms.

11. Common Problems & What to Do

Try these simple checks before contacting support.

Firebase data is still loading

  • Wait while the spinner/loading message is visible. The app may still be retrieving data.
  • Check your internet connection and try again after a short wait.
  • If loading never finishes, reopen the page once. If it continues, send feedback with the section name and approximate time.

Notes or Important Questions are not visible

  • Confirm you selected the correct branch, semester and subject.
  • Wait for loading to complete; do not assume an empty list means no content before loading finishes.
  • Some content may not have been published yet. Report the exact missing subject/file.

PDF download fails

  • Check device storage and the browser's download permission.
  • Retry the download and look in the Downloads folder.
  • If a print dialog opens, choose Save as PDF and check A4 preview.

Course/video access issue

  • Confirm that you are logged into the correct account and course approval is complete.
  • Check internet speed and retry the video player.
  • If payment was made, keep the transaction reference and contact support rather than paying again immediately.

Login or password issue

  • Double-check the email address and password for typing mistakes.
  • Use Forgot Password? and check Spam/Junk for the reset email.
  • Never share your password or reset link with anyone.
When reporting a problem: Tell support which page you were on, which button you tapped, what you expected, what actually happened, and the exact error message. A screenshot of the error can help, but hide private information first.

14. A Simple Study Routine Using the App

Combine resources to build a steady and useful learning habit.

  1. Learn: Open the notes or lesson for one topic and understand the core concepts.
  2. Revise: Read the corresponding Important Questions and attempt them without looking at the notes first.
  3. Practise: Take a relevant Test Series test when one is available.
  4. Review: Check your result/analysis, list mistakes and revisit the weak topic.
  5. Improve: Share constructive feedback if a resource is missing or a feature is not working.
  6. Stay consistent: Use the app regularly, track your progress and help classmates discover useful study resources.
The benefit comes from active learning: Use the app as a study companion, not just a place to download files. Understand concepts, practise questions and revise mistakes.
